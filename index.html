About 关于

I am an undergraduate in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at the College of Mathematics and Statistics, Northwest Normal University. My primary academic interests include nonlinear differential equations, boundary value problems, and complex analysis. 我是西北师范大学数学与统计学院数学与应用数学专业本科生，主要学术兴趣包括非线性微分方程、边值问题与复分析。

In my studies and research, I focus on understanding and exploring mathematical theory. Through literature reading, theoretical derivation, independent thinking, and the assistance of artificial intelligence tools, I continue to deepen my understanding of mathematical problems. I currently focus on differential equations and mathematical analysis, and have obtained some preliminary results in related problems. Looking ahead, I hope to explore the possibilities of AI-assisted mathematical research, pursue mathematical questions that interest me, and broaden my academic horizons. 在学习与研究中，我注重对数学理论的理解与探索，通过文献阅读、理论推导、独立思考以及人工智能工具的辅助，不断深化对数学问题的认识。目前，我主要关注微分方程与数学分析相关领域，并在相关问题的研究中取得了一些初步成果。未来，我希望进一步探索人工智能辅助数学研究的可能性，持续深入研究感兴趣的数学问题，不断拓展学术视野。

Outside my studies, I actively participate in mathematical modeling and data mining competitions, and share mathematical knowledge and learning resources through Douyin and WeChat. I value clear communication, rigorous thinking, and exchanges with my peers. 学习之外，我积极参加数学建模与数据挖掘竞赛，也通过抖音和微信公众号分享数学知识与学习资源。我重视清晰的表达、严谨的思考，以及与同伴之间的交流。
